Good case studies follow a simple arc. Keep each part short and concrete.

## The challenge

What was hard for the client before? Use their words where you can.

## The approach

What you did, in the order you did it. Screenshots and quotes help here.

## The result

Numbers first, then the human outcome. Put the headline numbers in the `results` frontmatter
so they appear at the top of the page.
